Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers "Quiet Hours Automation", a private tool used only by the owner of the HushTide YouTube channel to upload and schedule videos to that channel.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses Google's YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload scope, only to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times on the owner's own channel.

What we store

The authorisation token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and we do not access any other Google data.

Viewers

People who watch HushTide videos are covered by YouTube's own privacy policy. This tool does not collect any information about viewers.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
